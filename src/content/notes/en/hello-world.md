## First, a place you can reach

This site began as a page with nothing but Hello World. The first version focuses on three things: introducing Saenai, bringing public projects together, and making room for future technical notes.

The home page is a calling card. Visitors can get a feel for the space before exploring the projects and notes.

## Content and pages grow separately

Projects and notes are stored as Markdown. Projects describe their purpose, technology tags, and repository links; notes record questions, processes, and conclusions. Both share the site layout, with separate archive and detail pages.

Adding an article or a project only requires adding a content file.

## A simple static architecture

The first version uses Astro. Pages are generated as HTML at build time and served through GitHub Pages. Article bodies and project descriptions do not depend on JavaScript in the browser; only the project category filters use a small script.

Styles follow consistent rules for color, spacing, and typography. Desktop and mobile share the same content with different layouts. The site has no accounts, database, or analytics tracking.

## Room for what comes next

The initial content consists of public projects and this note about building the site. Personal introductions, technical articles, and work showcases can grow within the existing structure.

The site’s code is also public on [GitHub](https://github.com/Saenai/Saenai.github.io), where you can learn more about its implementation.
