## Understanding starts with a sentence

Japanese Analyzer is an AI-powered Japanese sentence analysis project for Chinese-speaking learners. It organizes reading information around parts of speech, kana, romaji, Chinese definitions, and grammatical components.

## Different ways to explore a sentence

- Break down sentence structure and inspect words and grammatical components.
- Use a full-sentence translation to understand the context.
- Recognize Japanese text in images and then analyze it.
- Support learning with read-aloud audio and pronunciation annotations.
